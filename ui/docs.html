User guide · fictional examples

How Availazy works, in pictures.

Everything a coordinator needs: what happens to a message, what the tool can read, how review works and what the two exports contain. Every example comes from the fictional demo week.

01Overview

How it fits together

Three programs on one computer. The browser holds your review; a small Python server does the reading and the bookkeeping; Ollama runs the model. They talk only over 127.0.0.1.

One run, step by step

  1. 1Pastea week of messages and your roster
  2. 2Parsedated lines, repeats folded together
  3. 3ReadGemma copies phrases; rules cross-check
  4. 4Resolvepeople, days and times, by code
  5. 5Ledgerhistory per person per day
  6. 6You reviewaccept, reject, edit, mark reviewed
  7. 7Exportfinal sheet and review log

02Getting started

Getting started

git clone https://github.com/bipul724/availability-ledger
cd availability-ledger
python3 server.py
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765/app.
  2. Click Load fictional demo, or paste your own messages and roster.
  3. Pick the week's Monday and a reader, then press Suggest records.

Choose a reader

Model + rules

Both read every message. Where they disagree, the card says so. The default.

Needs Ollama

Model only

Gemma's suggestions on their own, no cross-check.

Needs Ollama

Rules only

A keyword baseline. Weaker, but works anywhere Python runs.

No model needed

For the model readers: install and start Ollama, then ollama pull gemma4:e2b-it-qat (about 4.3 GB). Needs Python 3.10+ on macOS or Linux; nothing to pip install.

03Your messages

Your messages

Paste text or upload a .txt. Any of these three line formats works, mixed if needed.

Plain

2026-11-02 09:41 | Maria Lopez | I can do Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

WhatsApp, Android

02/11/2026, 09:41 - Maria Lopez: I can do Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

WhatsApp, iPhone

[02/11/2026, 09:41:03] Maria Lopez: I can do Tuesday and Thursday mornings.
  • WhatsApp dates are read month-first unless you tick WhatsApp dates are day-first.
  • The roster is one name per line, up to 20 volunteers. Similar names are flagged, and a short name that fits several people is left for you.
  • The same sender sending the same words within 24 hours counts once, shown as “Same as m11”.
  • Times are read as written; the timezone field is stored but not used.

04What it understands

What it understands

Code, not the model, turns words into times. The planning day runs from 09:00 to 20:00, and a bare “2-5” means the afternoon.

Days depend on when the message was sent

PhraseSentRead as
“Tuesday”Mon 2 NovTue 10 Nov the planning week
“Next Tuesday”Tue 3 NovTue 10 Nov both readings agree
“Next Tuesday”Mon 9 Nov10 or 17 Nov? you pick
“11/12”Tue 3 NovThu 12 Nov
“Tomorrow morning”Tue 10 NovWed 11 Nov 09:00–12:00
“this Saturday”Wed 4 NovSat 7 Nov outside the week, not applied
“one day this weekend”Tue 10 Nov14 or 15 Nov? you pick

Week planned: Monday 9 to Sunday 15 November 2026 (the demo week). Each row is a unit test in tests/test_ledger.py.

05Reviewing

Reviewing

Every suggestion is a card beside its message, with the quote highlighted. Nothing reaches the final sheet until you accept it.

Keyboard

  • JKnext / previous
  • Aaccept
  • Rreject
  • Eedit
  • Mmark reviewed
  • Escleave a field

Shortcuts

  • Accept all suggestions without warnings accepts every pending card that has only grey badges.
  • Accept all and mark reviewed appears on a message only when all its cards are clean and fully resolved.

Badges on a card

Yellow: check this first

Which volunteer? The name matches more than one Which day? The message does not settle it Outside the selected week Could not read a time, so not applied Some words are not in this message Model and rules read this differently Added by the rules: possible cancellation the model missed

Grey: for information

A similar name is on the roster No time given Conditional Replaces this person's earlier answer for the day Edited by you

06Exports

Exports

The final sheet says plainly whether you can rely on it.

Final sheet

availability-final-2026-11-09-complete.csv

Accepted records only, with corrections and cancellations already applied. Use this one to plan shifts.

volunteer · date · start · end · status · condition · cell_state · sources

Review log

availability-log-2026-11-09.csv

Every reviewed record in message order, with its quote and warnings: the audit trail. An offer and its later cancellation are both rows, so it is not the sheet.

record_id · review · volunteer · date · start · end · status · … · warnings

In both files, a cell that a spreadsheet would run as a formula (starting with =, +, - or @) gets a leading apostrophe.

07Privacy and limits

Privacy and limits

  • Messages, roster and decisions stay in the browser tab and the local server. No account, no cloud database, no telemetry by default.
  • Opt-in tracing sends timings, token counts and the model name only, never text, names, quotes, prompts or replies.
  • The hosted replay serves only the fictional week with saved model outputs and refuses pasted messages.
  • Reloading the tab loses the review; saving a session isn't built yet.
  • It records availability, not shift assignments.
  • A real quote with invented day or time words is flagged, not held back.
  • All accuracy figures come from fictional messages; time saved has not been measured.
