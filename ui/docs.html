01Overview

How it fits together

Three programs on your own computer. The browser holds your review; a small Python server reads and resolves each request without saving a review session; Ollama runs the model. By default they talk only over 127.0.0.1. The optional Sentry tracing path is drawn separately below; the hosted fictional replay is explained in section 7.

Sources

server.py (routes, 127.0.0.1 binding), ledger/model.py (/api/chat, /api/tags), ledger/tracing.py, ui/app.js (state).

Sources

ui/app.js run(), resolveNow(), onClick(); server.py parse, suggest, resolve, export.

02Getting started

Getting started

git clone https://github.com/bipul724/availability-ledger
cd availability-ledger
python3 server.py
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765/app.
  2. Click Load fictional demo, or paste your own messages and roster.
  3. Pick the week's Monday and a reader, then press Suggest records.

Choose a reader

Sources

server.py _suggest; ledger/hybrid.py merge; ledger/rules.py extract; ledger/model.py extract (timeout 300 s), available_models (2 s); ui/app.js init.

For the model readers: install and start Ollama, then ollama pull gemma4:e2b-it-qat (a 4.3 GB download in our measurement). Python 3.10 or newer is the stated requirement; tested on 3.12 and 3.15. Nothing to pip install for the app itself.

03Your messages

Your messages

Paste text or upload a .txt export. The file picker also accepts .csv, but reads it as text: it must still contain one of these dated-line formats, not an arbitrary spreadsheet table. These formats can be mixed.

Plain

2026-11-02 09:41 | Maria Lopez | I can do Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

WhatsApp, Android

02/11/2026, 09:41 - Maria Lopez: I can do Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

WhatsApp, iPhone

[02/11/2026, 09:41:03] Maria Lopez: I can do Tuesday and Thursday mornings.
  • WhatsApp dates are read month-first unless you tick WhatsApp dates are day-first. The plain format is always year-month-day.
  • Times are read as written. The timezone field is stored but not used; nothing is converted.
  • A repeat is covered by its original: marking the original reviewed covers the copy too.
Sources

ledger/messages.py parse_messages, mark_duplicates, parse_roster; server.py MAX_VOLUNTEERS; ui/app.js run(); tests: tests/test_ledger.py Duplicates.

04What it understands

What it understands

A suggestion arrives as copied words: who, which day, what time, available or not, and a quote. Code, not the model, turns those words into a person, dates and hours. When the words do not settle it, the card asks you instead of guessing.

Sources

ledger/names.py match_person; ledger/resolve.py resolve_day, resolve_time; ledger/core.py resolve_claim (evidence gate), _span, build_ledger; tests: tests/test_ledger.py, tests/test_review_safeguards.py, tests/test_d31_fixes.py.

Times: phrases on the 09:00–20:00 planning day

A bare hour from 1 to 7 without am or pm, such as “2-5”, is read as the afternoon. Start and end of the planning day are fixed at 09:00 and 20:00.

Days depend on when the message was sent

PhraseSentRead as
“Tuesday”Mon 2 NovTue 10 Nov the planning week
“Next Tuesday”Tue 3 NovTue 10 Nov both readings agree
“Next Tuesday”Mon 9 Nov10 or 17 Nov? you pick
“11/12”Tue 3 NovThu 12 Nov
“Tomorrow morning”Tue 10 NovWed 11 Nov 09:00–12:00
“this Saturday”Wed 4 NovSat 7 Nov outside the week, not applied
“one day this weekend”Tue 10 Nov14 or 15 Nov? you pick

Week planned: Monday 9 to Sunday 15 November 2026 (the demo week). Each day in this table is checked by a unit test in tests/test_ledger.py.

05Reviewing

Reviewing

Every suggestion is a card beside its message, with the quote highlighted. Deciding a card and marking its message reviewed are two separate things. Accepting a card puts it into the final sheet's reconciliation; marking messages reviewed is what lets that sheet be called complete. Neither is needed to download it.

Sources

ui/app.js review(), onFieldChange, onClick (accept-mark, add), bulkEligible, acceptClean, onKey, buildCsv, failure handling in run(); ledger/core.py build_ledger (live claims); ledger/export.py confirmed_export; browser checks review_safeguards.mjs, extraction_failures.mjs, sheet_display.mjs.

Keyboard

  • JKnext / previous card
  • Aaccept
  • Rreject
  • Eedit the first field
  • Mmark the card's message reviewed
  • Escleave a field

Shortcuts do not fire while you type in a field. A message without cards can only be marked with its button.

Badges on a card (examples)

06Exports

Exports

Two separate files. The final sheet reconciles accepted records and labels whether review is complete. “Complete” records your decisions; it is not an independent guarantee that they are correct. The review log lists the source records separately.

Final sheet columns

volunteer · date · start · end · status · condition · cell_state · sources

Line 1 is a status line: format, week, status, unreviewed messages, pending count, blocking issues.

Review log columns

record_id · review · volunteer · date · start · end · status · condition · message_id · message_time · sender · excerpt · warnings

Sources

ledger/export.py confirmed_export, final_csv, BLOCKING; ui/app.js exportFinal, buildCsv, exportCsv; spec docs/specs/final-export.md; tests: tests/test_export.py, browser csv_safety.mjs.

In both files, a cell that a spreadsheet would run as a formula (starting with =, +, -, @, a tab or a carriage return) gets a leading apostrophe.

07Privacy and limits

Privacy and limits

Where your data goes depends on which copy you use. The local app and the hosted replay take different paths; tracing is a third, optional path.

Sources

server.py (DEMO, check_demo, parse, saved_output, log_message, start-up order); ledger/tracing.py (scrub, clean_envelope); ui/app.js run(); docs/SENTRY_HANDOFF.md (received-data evidence); tests: tests/test_demo_mode.py, tests/test_tracing.py, browser session_rerun.mjs, demo_mode.mjs.

  • It records availability, not shift assignments.
  • A real quote with invented day or time words is flagged, not held back. Rules only can also mistake negation inside a condition for unavailability, such as “if it isn't raining”.
  • Times are read as written; the timezone is not used.
  • All accuracy figures come from fictional messages; time saved has not been measured.
